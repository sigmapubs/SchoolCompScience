Java Basics: Algorithms, Variables, Expressions, Input & Casting | AP CSA Unit 1 (1.1–1.5)

Start AP Computer Science A here. Topics 1.1 to 1.5: algorithms and your first Java program, compilers and errors, variables and primitive types, expressions and output (including integer division and %), assignment and Scanner input, and casting, ranges and overflow. Every lesson is followed by a step-by-step worked example, and the unit ends with a 'putting it together' example that uses everything at once. Each part ends with a practice problem. Unit 1 is split into 3 videos: Part 1 (1.1–1.5), Part 2 (1.6–1.10), Part 3 (1.11–1.15).
