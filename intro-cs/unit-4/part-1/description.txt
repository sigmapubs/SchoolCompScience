Write your first real programs in Python. This video covers Unit 4, lessons 1 to 6: print and comments, variables and assignment, numbers and arithmetic (including //, % and **), strings (joining, indexes, slices, methods), input from the user with int() and float(), and Booleans and comparisons with and, or and not. Each section ends with a practice problem. Every lesson is followed by a step-by-step worked example, and the unit ends with a 'putting it together' example that uses everything at once. Unit 4 is split into 2 videos: Part 1 (the basics) and Part 2 (decisions, loops, functions and lists).
