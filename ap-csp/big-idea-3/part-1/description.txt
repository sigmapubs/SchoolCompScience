AP CSP Big Idea 3 Part 1: Variables, Lists, MOD, Strings & Boolean Expressions

AP CSP Big Idea 3, Algorithms and Programming, part 1 of 5, in AP exam pseudocode. This video covers variables and the assignment arrow (and the swap trap), lists with indexes that start at 1, math expressions and MOD, strings with concatenation and substrings, and Boolean expressions with NOT, AND and OR. Each section ends with an AP-style practice question. Every lesson is followed by a step-by-step worked example, and the unit ends with a 'putting it together' example that uses everything at once. Big Idea 3 is the largest part of the AP exam, so it's split into 5 videos.
